# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MandatePay to be a welcoming project for people of every background and
experience level. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and respectful,
whether you are reporting a bug, proposing a design change, or asking a beginner
question.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good project:

- being kind and specific in reviews;
- assuming good intent while still challenging unsafe code;
- giving reasons and evidence, not just "this is wrong";
- respecting different levels of experience with Python, payments, AI and frontend
  work;
- accepting that a safety invariant may take priority over a clever feature.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments;
- sexualised language or imagery;
- personal or political attacks;
- publishing someone's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions;
- dismissing a security or safety concern without engaging with it.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat and any event where you represent the project.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project
maintainers through their GitHub profile or the repository's private reporting
channel. Do not use a public issue for a personal or sensitive report.

Maintainers will:

1. review the report in good faith and keep it confidential where possible;
2. take proportionate action, which may include a warning, content removal,
   temporary restriction or permanent ban;
3. avoid conflicts of interest, and recuse themselves when needed.

Because this is a small hackathon project, enforcement capacity is limited, but
we will take reports seriously and respond as promptly as we can.
