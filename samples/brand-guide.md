# Hearth & Crumb — brand handoff

## Files
- `logo-final.svg` — the vector master, version 1.0.

## Palette
- Espresso `#2B1B12`
- Golden crust `#E8B04B`
- Cream `#F6E7D3`

## Usage
The mark works on cream, kraft paper and dark espresso backgrounds. Keep clear space
around the disc equal to the height of the wordmark. Do not stretch the wordmark or
recolour the loaf.
